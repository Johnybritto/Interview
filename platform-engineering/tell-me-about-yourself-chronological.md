# Tell Me About Yourself — Chronological Platform Engineering Answer

## Purpose

Use this as a 2-minute answer for interviews when asked:

> Tell me about yourself.

The structure follows a clear career progression:

**Atos → Allstate → Fiserv**

**Infrastructure Operations → Automation → Containers → Kubernetes → Senior Platform Engineering**

---

## 2-Minute Answer

Hi, my name is Johny. I have around 15 years of experience across infrastructure, DevOps, cloud, and platform engineering.

I started my career with Atos as a NOC engineer, where I got my initial exposure to enterprise infrastructure, production monitoring, networking, and incident management. Over time, I moved deeper into storage and infrastructure operations, working on technologies like NetApp, EMC, SAN/NAS, backup, disaster recovery, and L2/L3 production support. That phase helped me build a strong foundation in troubleshooting, reliability, and enterprise infrastructure operations.

After Atos, I joined Allstate, where my responsibilities expanded beyond storage into Linux, Windows administration, automation, and platform support. I started automating repetitive operational and incident-resolution activities using scripting and tools like Python, Shell, Ansible, and Jenkins. That helped me gradually move from traditional infrastructure operations into DevOps and automation-focused work.

At Allstate, I then got the opportunity to move into the container platform team. That is where I started working on Pivotal Cloud Foundry, which was my first major exposure to containerized application platforms. Later, I expanded into Kubernetes-based platforms such as TKGI and EKS. Along with platform administration, I worked on application onboarding, CI/CD support, production troubleshooting, platform upgrades, PCF-to-Kubernetes migration, and helping application teams deploy and run their applications reliably.

Currently, I work with Fiserv as a Senior Platform Engineer. My current responsibilities include managing enterprise PCF and Kubernetes platforms across OpenShift, AKS, TKGI, and related platform services. I work on platform lifecycle management, GitOps-based deployments, observability, automation, backup and recovery, network policies, Calico, AVI load balancing, and production support.

Recently, I have also been involved in self-service platform capabilities, especially around Kubernetes Network Policies, where application teams can deploy required policies through a governed and automated workflow instead of depending on manual platform intervention.

Overall, my career has progressed from core infrastructure operations to automation, then to container platforms, and now to senior platform engineering. My strength is managing large-scale platforms, improving reliability, automating manual work, and helping application teams deploy and troubleshoot production workloads effectively.

---

## Speaking Notes

- Keep the flow chronological, but do not over-explain every company.
- Avoid filler words like "like", "basically", "over there", and "all those areas".
- Emphasize progression: infrastructure → automation → containers → Kubernetes/platform engineering.
- Close with your strengths: reliability, automation, large-scale platforms, and application team support.

## Short Closing Line

My strength is managing large-scale platforms, improving reliability, automating manual work, and helping application teams deploy and troubleshoot production workloads effectively.
